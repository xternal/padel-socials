Get your own Padel Socials

Padel Socials shows every padel social, americano and group class that still has a free place at the London Playtomic clubs you choose, on one page, sorted by time. Each copy is private to the person who sets it up. It's free and takes about five minutes.

  1. Tap Deploy your own copy and sign in to Vercel with GitHub. Both are free.
  2. When Vercel asks for PADEL_KEY, make up a long password. That's your key.
  3. When the deploy finishes, open your new site and type your key once. Your phone remembers it.
  4. Choose your clubs and level, and add the page to your home screen.

Questions

Which clubs does it cover?
The 30 London clubs on Playtomic, from Padel Social Club Earls Court and Rocks Lane Chiswick to Padel Zest, Padel Play London and S3 Padel. Pick up to 8 at a time.
Does it book for me?
No. It shows what has free places; each event opens in Playtomic, where you book as usual.
Why do I need my own copy?
Playtomic's terms allow personal, non-commercial use. Your copy reads their public club pages for you alone, so keep its link to yourself.
Is it approved by Playtomic?
No. Playtomic hasn't approved third-party tools that read its website and may limit or block them, so this can stop working at any time.
Where's the code?
github.com/xternal/padel-socials, free to use under the MIT licence. Ideas or problems: ideas@guzh.uk.

Not affiliated with Playtomic.
